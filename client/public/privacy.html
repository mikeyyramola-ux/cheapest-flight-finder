Privacy Policy

Fareloop · Last updated: October 9, 2026

Fareloop runs in your browser with no accounts and no server-side profile. Nothing here describes data we hold about you, because we do not hold any.

1. What is stored on your device

  • fareloop.alertChannel — your price-alert preference
  • fareloop.sortMode — your result sort choice
  • theme — light/dark preference
  • manus-runtime-user-info — a runtime id used by the app shell

Clear them any time via your browser's site-data controls. Clearing them also resets your consent choice, so the ad banner will ask again.

2. Analytics

A cookieless umami instance records page views and referrers only. It sets no advertising cookies and builds no cross-site profile, and we do not use it for ad personalisation.

3. Advertising

Fareloop does not currently show advertising: no ad scripts (including Google AdSense) are loaded and no advertising cookies or identifiers are set. If advertising is ever added, this policy and the page itself will say so before any ad script runs.

4. Affiliate links

Flight links to partner sites (*.tpk.lu — Aviasales, Kiwi) may earn Fareloop a commission at no extra cost to you. They are clearly labelled and open in a new tab with rel="sponsored nofollow noopener". Partners set their own cookies under their own policies once you click.

5. Prices and third-party content

Fares, schedules and availability come from third-party partner sites and can change before you pay. We do not control how those sites process data; their policies apply on their side.

6. No sale of data

We do not sell or share personal information for money, and there is no account or server-side profile to export or delete.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar), you may have rights to access, delete, correct, or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time — withdrawing is as simple as clearing site data for this domain, which turns ads off again.

8. Changes

We'll update this page and change the "Last updated" date above when the policy changes.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: Fareloop — support@fareloop.in
